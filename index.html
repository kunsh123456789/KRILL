          How to play

          Each round gives you a category, like “Name a farm animal.” Type any valid answer before your air runs out.

          Being correct gets you in. Being rare gets you deep. Obvious answers earn a little; answers few people think of earn a lot.

            Tips

            • Small typos in longer words still count, and so do plurals and common alternate names.
            • Daily Dive: everyone gets the same 7 prompts, once per day. Unlimited: play as much as you like.
            • Close the tab whenever you like. Your dive is saved and resumes where you left off.
